Methodology & Sources

What this tracks: The official Mietspiegel base rent table values for "mittlere Wohnlage" (middle residential location category), for apartments in the 60–80 sqm range with typical building age cohorts (1965–1974). Values represent the net cold rent per square meter as published in each city's official "qualifizierter Mietspiegel."

Data sources: Values are extracted from official city Mietspiegel PDFs published by each city's administration (Senatsverwaltung, Stadtplanungsamt, or equivalent). Each city's most recent edition links to its official source.

Limitations: Mietspiegel tables differ in structure between cities and across years. Some values are interpolated from the closest comparable building-age cohort. Only "qualifizierte Mietspiegel" editions are tracked (not "einfache" or "Tabellenmietspiegel"). Publication years vary per city — some use odd years (Berlin, Hamburg), others even years (Frankfurt, Leipzig).

Next update: Data will be refreshed as new Mietspiegel editions are published. Berlin, Munich, and Hamburg are expected to publish 2025 editions in 2026.
