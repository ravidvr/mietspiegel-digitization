Your neighborhood is your rent

Same building age, same size band — a pre-1918 flat in guter Lage rents 22% more than one in einfacher Lage. That's €141/month extra on a 70 m² flat.

+22%
Gut vs einfach Lage premium (35-40 m², pre-1918, official table)
The neighborhood matters — and the spread differs by city. In Essen, the gut/einfach spread is 2.2× — the most unequal city in the dataset. In Dresden, it's 1.05× — the most equitable.

What €1,200 buys

At €1,200/month cold rent, you can afford 99 m² at market rates. An existing tenant in the same building might be paying that for 151 m². The question isn't "can you afford Berlin?" — it's "when did you arrive?"

99 m²
At market rate vs 151 m² for existing tenants
At €12.07/m² (market), €1,200 gets you 99 m². At €7.97/m² (census), the same money covers 151 m². The difference is 52 m² — an entire room.
