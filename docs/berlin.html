🏠 Berlin Rent Map — How to use

Block-by-block rent data from three independent sources, layered on one map.

🎯 What you're looking at

  • Heatmap — Immoscout24 market rents (2021–2025). Red = expensive, green = cheaper.
  • Census toggle — What residents actually pay (Zensus 2022). Usually much lower.
  • District boundaries — 12 Bezirke with average rents. Hover to see details.

🔍 How to use

  • Click anywhere on the map to see rent data for that exact spot — market vs census comparison.
  • Search by address, building name, or PLZ using the search bar above the map.
  • Toggle Census to switch between market asking rents and what current tenants pay.
  • Hover districts to see average rents. Click for full breakdown.

📊 The key insight

Market rents (€12.07/m² avg) are 51% higher than what current tenants pay (€7.97/m² census). New tenants pay dramatically more than long-term residents.
