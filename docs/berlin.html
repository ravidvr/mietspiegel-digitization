🏠 Berlin Rent Map — How to use

Block-by-block rent data from three independent sources, layered on one map.

🎯 What you're looking at

  • Heatmap — Immoscout24 market rents (2023–2025). Dark blue = cheaper, orange/red = more expensive. Colour-blind-safe.
  • Census toggle — What residents actually pay (Zensus 2022). Usually much lower.
  • District boundaries — 12 Bezirke with average rents. Hover to see details.
  • 💶 Budget Check — set your monthly budget and see exactly where in Berlin you can afford to live.
  • 🔍 Rent Check — enter any apartment's details and instantly see if the rent is fair or overpriced.

🔍 How to use

  • Click anywhere on the map to see rent data for that exact spot — market vs census comparison.
  • Search by address, building name, or PLZ using the search bar above the map.
  • Toggle Census to switch between market asking rents and what current tenants pay.
  • Hover districts to see average rents. Click for full breakdown.

📊 The key insight

Market rents (€12.07/m² avg) are 51% higher than what current tenants pay (€7.97/m² census). New tenants pay dramatically more than long-term residents.

Can I afford Berlin?

Set your budget and see exactly where you can live.

1

Newest Flats Cost 93% More

+93%newest vs post-war stock, same size

In the official Mietspiegel 2023 table, a 40-60 m² flat in einfacher Lage costs €12.37/m² if built 2003-2017 — but only €6.41/m² in a 1973-1990 East Berlin building. Same size, same Lage: the age of the walls nearly doubles the rent.

2

The Two Berlins

+51%more for new tenants

If you signed a lease last month, you pay €4.10/m² more (net cold rent) than your neighbor who's been there 5 years. On a 70 m² flat: €287/month — €3,444/year. The city is splitting into two populations: those who got in, and those who didn't.

3

The Market Runs Ahead of the Official Table

9/12districts above the city-wide market average

Nine of 12 Berlin districts have market asking rents above the city-wide Immoscout average (€10.79/m²), covering 286,428 addresses. Charlottenburg-Wilmersdorf leads at +18.6%. Note: none of these district averages exceeds the Mietpreisbremse cap of 110% of the Mietspiegel — the cap operates per apartment, not per district.

4

Your Neighborhood Is Your Rent

+22%gut vs einfach Lage, same band

Same building age, same size band — a 35-40 m² pre-1918 flat rents for €9.08/m² in einfacher Lage but €11.09/m² in guter Lage. That's €141/month extra on a 70 m² flat. The neighborhood matters.

5

New Walls, Higher Bills

+84%new vs Altbau, gut Lage

A 65-85 m² flat in guter Lage costs €8.45/m² in a pre-1918 Altbau — but €15.58/m² in a 2016-2022 building. €499/month for the privilege of new walls.

6

What €1,200 Buys

99 m²at market rate vs 151 m² for existing tenants

At €1,200/month cold rent, you can afford 99 m² at market rates. An existing tenant in the same building might be paying that for 151 m². The question isn't "can you afford Berlin?" — it's "when did you arrive?"

All values: net cold rent (Nettokaltmiete). Immoscout24 2023–2025 · Zensus 2022 census rents · Official Mietspiegel 2024 · Last updated: 2025-07
